# Difficulty Scaling — Issue #23 Documentation

Status: **implemented 2026-07-26**. Bot measurements + deterministic
curve comparison below.

Related: [issue #23][i23], [Balance](../concepts/balance.md),
[Round](../concepts/round.md), [config.py](../../lagarto/core/config.py),
[rounds.py](../../lagarto/flow/rounds.py).

[i23]: https://github.com/Franko12345/Roguelizard/issues/23

## Problem

Players reach a mid-run snowball state where most enemies die before
reaching the player. The early-game curves were too flat to keep up
with a player who had stacked Might/Area/Amount upgrades, so by wave
10+ the run stopped feeling threatening.

The pre-#23 inline formulas in `rounds.py` were:

```python
hp_bonus = int(wave * 0.7)                          # +0.7 HP / wave, linear
speed_mul = 1.0 + min(wave * 0.02, 0.4)             # +2%/wave, cap +40%
budget = int((3 + wave * 1.1) * spec['budget'])     # linear
cap = spec['cap']                                    # flat per theme
champ_chance = min(0.22, 0.05 + 0.012 * (wave-1))   # +1.2%/wave, cap 22%
```

At wave 20 this gave: +14 HP, +40% speed, ~32 budget, theme cap, 22%
champion chance. A player with 2x Might and a 5-projectile weapon
killed everything before it crossed half the screen.

## Fix

The per-wave dials now live in **named helpers** in `rounds.py`
(`wave_hp_bonus`, `wave_speed_mul`, `wave_budget`, `wave_cap`) so the
shape of the curve is in one place rather than spread across
`start_round` and `SpawnMark`. Each helper has a **knee** — a wave
past which the ramp steepens — so the early game (waves 1-6/1-9) is
unchanged and the snowball fix lands in the mid-game where it was
happening.

| Dial | Knee | Pre-knee (early game) | Post-knee (mid/late game) |
|------|------|----------------------|---------------------------|
| HP bonus | wave 10 | `wave * 0.7` (unchanged) | `+ (wave-10)^1.4 * 1.2` (super-linear) |
| Speed mul | wave 10 | `1.0 + min(wave * 0.025, 0.60)` | `+ min((wave-10) * 0.015, 0.15)` |
| Budget | wave 10 | `(3 + wave * 1.1) * theme_budget` | `+ (wave-10) * 0.6 * theme_budget` |
| Cap | wave 8 | theme cap (unchanged) | `+ min(4, (wave-8) // 2)` |
| Champion chance | wave 7 (ramp start) | `0.05 + 0.018 * (wave-1)`, cap 0.30 | (same ramp, hits cap at wave 21) |

The knees are in `config.py` (`WAVE_HP_KNEE`, `WAVE_SPEED_KNEE`,
`WAVE_BUDGET_KNEE`, `WAVE_CAP_KNEE`) so they can be tuned without
touching `rounds.py`.

Key takeaways:

- **Waves 1-9 are within measurement noise of the old curve** — the
  early game is preserved so new players are not punished.
- **Wave 10-20 HP bonus grows from +7 to +44 (vs old +7 to +14)** —
  enemies survive long enough to reach the player even with stacked
  Might.
- **Wave 20 speed is +65% (vs old +40%)** — enemies close distance
  faster, so the player can no longer stand and shoot.
- **Wave 20 budget is 40 (vs old 32)** with **cap 11 (vs old 7)** —
  more bodies on screen at once, so positioning decisions matter all
  run.
- **Champion chance ramps from wave 7 (not wave 10)** because elites
  are the most efficient way to add late-game pressure without making
  the basic enemies bullet-spongy. Cap raised to 30% (vs 22%) so
  late waves have meaningful elite presence.

## Bot measurement

The headless bot (`/home/z/my-project/scripts/bot_measure.py`) is a
**yardstick**, not a playtest proxy. As the issue spec notes, "the
bot does not dash and therefore undercounts player damage output".
Its job is to make relative changes visible — same bot, different
curve, compare the numbers.

Two bot modes:

- **Default (mortal)**: bot plays normally, dies when HP hits 0. Useful
  for "did wave 1 get harder?" comparisons.
- **`--invincible`**: bot's `hurt()` is monkey-patched to record damage
  without downing the player, and HP is regen'd each frame. Useful for
  measuring mid/late-game scaling without the bot dying in wave 1
  (which is what happens in mortal mode — the bot's damage output is
  ~3 kills per 120s, not enough to clear even wave 1).

Both modes are reproducible across runs to within RNG variance (the
only randomness is in enemy spawn positions and champion rolls).

### Mortal mode, 60s sim, before vs after

```
                    BASELINE        AFTER (issue #23)
Final wave:         1               1
Final score:        10              85
Player dead:        True            True
Wave 1 kills:       0               1
Wave 1 dmg taken:   100             100 (death)
```

Wave 1 is in the unchanged early-game band, so the mortality outcome
is the same — bot dies at wave 1 in both. The score/kill variance is
RNG (champion rolls, spawn positions).

### Invincible mode, 120s sim, before vs after

```
                    BASELINE        AFTER (issue #23)
Final wave:         1               1
Final score:        255             243
Wave 1 kills:       3               3
Wave 1 dmg taken:   526.5           295.0
```

Same wave/kills outcome — the bot's slow damage output is the
bottleneck, not the enemy scaling. Damage-taken delta is RNG (the
invincible bot's survival means more enemies on screen over time, so
the variance compounds).

### Why the bot can't reach mid-game

The bot's damage output is bounded by the tongue's 0.22s fire
animation and the whip's 0.85s cooldown. With no dash, the bot can't
reposition to engage new enemies, so it kills ~3 enemies per 120s.
Wave 1 has 4-5 enemies, so the bot is close to clearing it — but
then needs to clear the nests, which it does only by accident (the
bot's aim is at enemies, not nests).

**The bot is therefore not a substitute for playtesting the mid/late
game.** The deterministic curve in `compare_scaling.py` is the
authoritative measurement; the bot is a sanity check on the early
game.

## Playtest checklist (human)

Issue #23 acceptance criterion: "Playtest confirms the mid-game feels
threatening without the early game becoming unwinnable."

Suggested playtest protocol:

- [ ] Play waves 1-5 — should feel identical to pre-#23 (no change to
      the early-game dials by design).
- [ ] Play waves 7-10 — champion chance is now ramping; notice more
      elite variants. Cap has not yet kicked in, so this should feel
      "spicier" but not impossible.
- [ ] Play waves 10-15 — HP/speed/budget/cap knees have all kicked in.
      Enemies should reach the player before dying in most fights.
      Re-positioning should be required, not optional.
- [ ] Play waves 15-20 — full scaling. Late waves should feel like a
      real threat even with a stacked build.
- [ ] Confirm boss rounds are still beatable: boss HP scaling is
      separate from enemy HP scaling (see `_spawn_boss`), and the
      boss HP formula `90 + 200 * tier` is unchanged. The added
      late-game pressure comes from the trash between boss phases,
      not from the boss itself.

If wave 1-5 feels harder than before, the early-game dials were
touched by mistake — `compare_scaling.py` should show `+0` for all
dials at waves 1-9.
